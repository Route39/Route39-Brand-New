Delete Your Account

Route39 Pilot — an app by Attendy Technologies Pvt Ltd

You can request deletion of your Route39 Pilot driver account and associated data directly from within the app. Please follow the steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Route39 Pilot app and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile section.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request when prompted.

What Gets Deleted

Once your deletion request is confirmed, we remove your personal profile information — including your name, email address, profile photo, and account credentials — from our active systems.

What May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained for a limited period where required to meet our legal, tax, and regulatory (KYC) obligations, such as government identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, RC), trip and earnings history, and transaction records. This data is retained only as long as necessary or as required under applicable law, and is not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.
